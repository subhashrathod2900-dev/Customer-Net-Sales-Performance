
📊AtliQ Hardwares – #CustomerNetSalesPerformance

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes AtliQ Hardwares' customer net sales performance in India from 2019 to 2021.

The analysis focuses on comparing customer-wise net sales and understanding how sales changed from 2020 to 2021.
🎯 Objective
The main objective of this project is to:
Analyze customer-wise net sales performance.
Compare sales between 2019, 2020, and 2021.
Identify customers with significant sales growth.
Understand the overall growth in customer net sales.
Present the analysis in a simple and easy-to-understand format.

🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
Microsoft Excel
Data Analysis
Data Cleaning
Pivot Tables
Excel Formulas
Data Visualization
Business Analysis

📊 Key Metrics
The analysis covers:
Customer-wise Net Sales
Net Sales 2019
Net Sales 2020
Net Sales 2021
2021 vs 2020 Sales Growth

📈 Key Insights
Overall Sales Growth
AtliQ Hardwares' total customer net sales increased from:
2019: 30.8M
2020: 49.8M
2021: 161.3M
The report shows 324.0% growth in 2021 compared with 2020

📌 Conclusion
This project helped me understand how to analyze customer-level sales data, compare year-wise performance, calculate growth, and communicate business insights using Excel.
